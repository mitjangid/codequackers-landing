Frequently Asked Questions

How does Nocturne differ from generic POS systems like Petpooja or Posist for nightclubs? +

Generic restaurant systems like Petpooja lack the velocity and compliance needed for high-energy nightlife. Nocturne is engineered specifically for pubs and nightclubs: it features instant running tabs across standing bars and VIP zones, dedicated touchscreen Bar Order Ticket (BOT) dispense displays, milliliter-level peg depletion, integrated door entry cover charges, and automatic generation of official state excise registers (FL-9 and Form 16).

How does Nocturne manage running bar tabs across tables and VIP areas? +

Tabs attach dynamically to tables, private booths, standing bar groups, or RFID wristbands. Bartenders can add drinks, transfer guests across floors, and split or merge bills without losing line-item history.

How do Bar Order Tickets (BOT) route to bartender screens? +

Drink orders instantly fire to touchscreen bartender displays arranged by drink category (cocktails, beers, spirits). Orders show recipe guides, glassware type, and peg deduction measurements.

Does Nocturne generate Indian state excise FL-9 and Form 16 registers? +

Yes. Nocturne automatically generates state-compliant excise books, bottle intake ledgers, breakage logs, and daily physical vs. POS pour reconciliation reports in official FL-9 and Form 16 formats.

How does Nocturne separate State Liquor VAT from restaurant food taxes? +

Alcoholic beverages are taxed at State Liquor VAT rates (10%–25%), while food and mocktails carry 5% restaurant GST. Nocturne computes and isolates both taxes on printed guest folios and daily Z-reports.

Does Nocturne manage door cover charges and entry passes? +

Yes. The Door module handles stag/couple entry covers, VIP minimum spends, and RFID wristband balance top-ups, redeemable against food and beverage tabs across any bar counter inside the venue.
