Frequently Asked Questions

Why is PowerTrack a better alternative to GymMaster, Mindbody, and GymDesk? +

Unlike GymMaster and Mindbody, which lock fitness clubs into proprietary hardware, charge punitive per-member SaaS fees that escalate as your gym expands, and require expensive third-party SMS add-ons, PowerTrack provides open biometric API integration (eSSL, Mantra, ZKTeco), transparent flat pricing with zero per-member penalties, native Indian WhatsApp Business API reminders with 1-click UPI payments, and built-in Indian diet/macro planning without additional subscriptions.

Does PowerTrack support biometric fingerprint attendance devices in India? +

Yes. PowerTrack integrates with biometric fingerprint, RFID card, and facial recognition terminals (eSSL, Realtime, Mantra) via LAN/USB webhooks, logging member check-ins in real time and automatically alerting staff when expired memberships attempt entry.

How do automated WhatsApp renewal reminders work in PowerTrack? +

PowerTrack connects with the official WhatsApp Business API to send personalized renewal notifications 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day prior to membership expiry, with embedded UPI payment links for instant renewal without manual staff calls.

Can PowerTrack manage multi-branch gym franchises? +

Yes. Gym owners can manage multiple franchise locations from a central dashboard, review consolidated member retention and collections, and enable cross-branch check-in privileges across authorized facilities.

Does PowerTrack handle personal trainer commissions and payroll? +

Yes. PowerTrack features a dedicated trainer portal that tracks client personal training (PT) sessions, computes tiered trainer commissions upon package sales, and generates monthly trainer payroll disbursements.

Can gym members access workout routines and diet charts on mobile? +

Yes. Members receive a mobile Progressive Web App (PWA) to track trainer-assigned workout schedules, calorie and macronutrient diets, BMI progress graphs, and digital membership QR codes.
