Sub-Second Barcode Counter Billing

Rapid keyboard-first and laser scanner checkout. Print thermal receipts or A4 invoices in seconds without touching the mouse.

🏛️

Direct GST E-Invoicing & E-Way Bills

Generate Government IRN QR codes and E-Way bills directly within the invoice screen with a single click. Zero manual portal copy-pasting.

🏢

Multi-Branch Stock Transfers

Ship goods between city warehouses and retail outlets with transfer notes, transit tracking, and automatic receiving reconciliation.

📒

Customer & Supplier Khata (Ledger)

Maintain digital party ledgers for credit customers. Automated payment balance reminders on WhatsApp with integrated UPI payment links.

🏷️

Batch, Expiry & Serial Numbering

Track FMCG, pharmaceutical, and electronic products by batch number, MRP, and manufacturing/expiry dates with FIFO picking.

🌐

Public Shop Discovery Integration

Every business running on BillNBin is discoverable on our public directory where customers find real stores with verified products and phone numbers.
