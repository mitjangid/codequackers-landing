Frequently Asked Questions

Why do restro-bars and pubs choose Sarur over generic restaurant POS like Petpooja? +

Standard restaurant POS systems like Petpooja were designed for food billing and treat alcohol as generic menu items. Sarur was engineered specifically for high-volume bars and hybrid dining: it natively separates 5% Food GST from 20%+ State Liquor VAT on a single guest bill, generates Bar Order Tickets (BOT) directly to the rail, tracks liquor inventory down to 30ml/60ml pegs and cocktail recipes, and maintains state excise compliance logs (FL-9/Form 16) that food-first POS platforms cannot handle.

How does Sarur split Restaurant Food GST and State Liquor VAT on the same bill? +

Under Indian tax law, alcoholic beverages are outside GST and subject to state-specific VAT (typically 10%–25%) and state excise duty, while food and non-alcoholic items fall under 5% restaurant GST (or 18% with ITC). Sarur automatically isolates food from bar categories, computing and displaying both tax lines clearly on the customer thermal bill and daily reconciliation ledgers without manual calculation.

How does the dual-stream KOT and BOT pass routing work? +

When an order is punched on the POS terminal or submitted via guest table QR, Sarur automatically routes drink items to the bartender's Bar Order Ticket (BOT) display screen on the rail, while food items fire instantly to kitchen pass slip printers and digital KDS screens. Both stations can mark items prepared independently.

Can Sarur operate for a pure-vegetarian dining restaurant without a bar? +

Yes. Sarur includes back-office operational toggles that let you switch into Pure Veg mode (hiding all non-veg indicators and diet filters) and disable Bar Mode (disabling drink tickets, private bar menus, and liquor tax lines), converting the system into a streamlined dining restaurant POS.

Does the POS work offline during internet outages? +

Yes. Engineered as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) with local edge persistence, the POS terminal allows cashiers and captains to take orders, print bills, and collect tender during network blackouts. All offline transactions queue locally and sync with the cloud as soon as connection is restored.

How does cellar inventory tracking handle pegs and cocktail recipes? +

Sarur tracks alcohol inventory down to the milliliter. Every time a drink or cocktail is rung up, the exact recipe quantities (such as 30ml/60ml pegs, mixers, and syrups) automatically deduct from the cellar stock ledger, preventing spillage, unrecorded pours, and inventory discrepancies.
