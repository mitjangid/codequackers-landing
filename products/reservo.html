Frequently Asked Questions & Comparisons

How is Reservo a better alternative to Playo, Hudle, and KheloMore?

+

Aggregator platforms like Playo, Hudle, and KheloMore take heavy cuts of 15% to 25% on every booking and keep control of your customer database. Reservo gives turf owners 100% direct bookings with 0% commissions. You get a branded booking website with instant direct UPI payments, 8-minute anti-clash slot locks, dynamic peak/night lighting charges, and automated WhatsApp passes without sacrificing your margins.

Can Reservo prevent double bookings across offline phone calls and online visitors?

+

Yes. Reservo’s live slot engine locks slots for 8 minutes the moment an online user enters the checkout flow. Front-desk staff can instantly block slots for walk-in or phone reservations from the management console, syncing instantly across all customer devices to eliminate double bookings.
