Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose BhavTrack over legacy bullion terminals and platforms like Augmont? +

Legacy bullion terminals often require slow desktop installations, clunky Java clients, or lock jewellers into high brokerage fees and proprietary hardware. BhavTrack delivers sub-50ms tick dispatch over lightweight binary WebSockets directly in the browser, turns standard Smart TVs into 4K showroom display boards with custom purity conversions, and allows jewellers to deal directly via WhatsApp with zero commission deductions.

How low is the streaming latency in BhavTrack? +

BhavTrack streams tick-by-tick market benchmark updates in under 50 milliseconds using binary WebSockets, ensuring jewellers and traders always view live price movement without refreshing.

Can BhavTrack power jewellery showroom TV display boards? +

Yes. BhavTrack runs seamlessly on any Smart TV, Firestick, or tablet in 4K resolution, auto-computing 18K, 22K, and 24K retail prices with custom dealer margins.
