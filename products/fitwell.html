Fitwell

Wedding Wear Rental, Tailoring & Wardrobe Management ERP
Live in Production Launch Live Platform ↗

Rent. Stitch. Celebrate. Fitwell is the mobile-first operating system designed specifically for Indian wedding wear rental showrooms, custom tailors, and readymade sellers. Built to withstand peak wedding season chaos with live public availability, advance deposit tracking, damage deductions, tailor piece-rate job cards, and multi-event wedding party coordination.

Real-Time Rental Inventory Calendar

Visual scheduling across bridal lehengas, groom sherwanis, tuxedos, and wedding jewelry. Tracks outfit status from reservation to trial, dispatch, return inspection, and dry-cleaning cycles.

Advance Tokens & Security Deposits

Eliminate revenue leaks with automated deposit ledgers. Hold security deposits, itemize cleaning or tear deductions upon return, and execute one-click UPI customer refunds.

Tailor Job Cards & Piece-Rate Ledger

Paperless measurement cards for master tailors and karigars. Track alteration checklists, promised completion dates, and automate piece-rate wage payouts without manual tallying.

Coordinate bride side, groom side, bridesmaids, and groomsmen outfits on a unified styling board. Match color palettes, themes, and event timelines effortlessly.

Engineered on modern PHP 8.3 / Laravel and edge CDN caching, ensuring sub-second response times even during auspicious muhurat booking rushes.

Architecture & Tech Stack

Backend:Laravel 11 / PHP 8.3 (High-performance queue workers & financial ledgers)
Frontend & UI:Tailwind CSS, Blade SSR, Vite build pipeline & dual-mode theme engine
Mobile App Layer:Installable PWA with ServiceWorker caching & Web Manifest
Edge CDN & Security:Hostinger Edge (HTTP/3), HSTS, CSP, and strict same-origin isolation
Roles & RBAC:Multi-role permissions for Showroom Owners, Master Tailors & Clients

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose Fitwell over generic billing tools like Vyapar or Excel?
Generic billing tools only track simple sales. They cannot track return deadlines, dry cleaning turnaround buffers, rental security deposits, customer damage deductions, or bespoke measurement cards. Fitwell is custom-engineered for the specific workflows of garment rental and tailoring.
Can multiple showroom staff and tailors use it simultaneously?
Yes. Fitwell features role-based access control (RBAC). Floor sales staff can book dates and take measurements, counter managers handle billing and refunds, and tailors access their assigned alteration queue on their own phones.
How do customers check outfit availability?
Fitwell provides public availability calendars so wedding clients and brides can browse available dates before walking into the showroom, saving hours of counter consultation.
