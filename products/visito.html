A Purchase-List Inbox, Not a Cart Maze

Indian neighbourhood customers don't navigate 25-step shopping carts — they send handwritten lists or voice notes. Visito lets customers type, tap catalog items, photograph their paper notebook, or record audio. The merchant marks each item as available, out of stock, or swapped with a verified total.

An Appointment Book That Knows Real Hours

Slots calculate the exact mathematical intersection of shop hours and staff rosters minus lunch breaks, approved leave, and existing appointments. Features automatic 5-minute checkout holds to eliminate double-booking, service buffer buffers, and 24h/2h automated reminders.

🚚

Tracked Self-Delivery with OTP Proof

Keep your delivery staff without paying 30% aggregator platform commissions. Configure radius and pincode delivery zones, assign drivers with a single tap, and verify hand-offs at the door using customer OTP codes or photo proof.

Khata Ledger That Adds Itself Up

Replace paper ledger diaries with a searchable credit khata. Track credit given, payments received, and pending balances per customer. Built-in UPI QR generation and printable thermal bills live on the exact same screen.

Catalogue Shelf Agreement

One tap flips any shelf item to unavailable. Customers stop walking over in the heat for items you ran out of at noon, and get notified instantly the minute fresh stock arrives.

Hyperlocal Directory & Verified Reviews

Showcase shop name, walking distance, live open/closed status, catalog items, and verified customer reviews with owner replies. Get discovered by customers two lanes away who didn't know your service existed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Visito a purchase-list inbox instead of a traditional cart? +

Every Indian neighbourhood shop runs on WhatsApp messages, phone calls, and handwritten paper slips. Traditional shopping carts force customers to search, select variants, and click through 20 checkout screens, leading to massive cart abandonment. Visito allows customers to simply write, snap a photo, or dictate what they need. The shopkeeper reviews the list on a single screen, taps available or substituted, quotes an exact price, and closes the sale in minutes.

How does Visito handle appointments for salons, barbers, and clinics? +

Visito's appointment engine evaluates the merchant's operating week against staff-specific working hours, subtracting mandatory lunch breaks, leaves, public holidays, and booked slots. It places a 5-minute temporary hold while the client completes booking, preventing chair double-booking even during peak weekend rushes. Automated reminders fire at 24 hours and 2 hours to drastically cut customer no-shows.

Are there any commissions or hidden platform fees? +

None. Visito does not take a percentage of order totals or delivery fees. Payments can be settled directly to the merchant's business bank account via UPI QR codes or cash on delivery. Merchants retain 100% of their margins and full customer data ownership.

Does Visito require customers or merchants to download an app from Play Store? +

No app store installation is needed. Visito is a Progressive Web App (PWA) with a single service worker serving both the customer storefront (/) and the business console (/biz). Customers and shop owners can install it directly to their home screen with a single tap, enabling offline browsing, push notifications, and sub-100ms navigation.
