One App Runs Your Whole Water Distribution Operation

🏢

Clients & Corporate Contracts

Full profiles with GSTIN, billing address, contract dates, auto-renewal switches, and status badges. Search in milliseconds and view every customer balance at a single glance.

🏷️

Serialized Dispenser Fleet Tracking

Every dispenser tracked by serial number — assigned, idle, or under maintenance. Log security deposits, machine refunds, sanitization schedules, and filter replacements.

🔄

Deliveries & Container Control

Log 20L can refills in seconds with search-inside customer dropdowns. Track issued vs returned containers to the rupee so bottles never go missing in the field.

💳

Bills That Write Themselves

Auto-generate monthly recurring GST invoices combining fixed machine rental fees and consumed 20L water cans. Send automated PDF bills to clients on WhatsApp.

💰

Payments & Advance Ledger

Track cash, UPI, and bank transfers with complete payment history. Manage advance balances, partial credits, and refundable asset deposits without manual ledgers.

📊

Reports & Profit & Loss

Accountant-ready monthly reporting: bottle consumption by route, depot stock levels, revenue per dispenser asset, receivables aging, and automated nightly backups.
