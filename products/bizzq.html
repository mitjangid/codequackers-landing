Frequently Asked Questions & Comparisons

How is BizzQ a better alternative to WordPress, Webflow, and Wix?

+

Traditional website platforms like WordPress suffer from bloated themes, expensive plugin renewals, security exploits, and sluggish 4-second loading times. Webflow has a steep learning curve and expensive hosting tiers. BizzQ provides a blazing-fast (98+ Lighthouse score) website builder with built-in markdown blogging, integrated appointment calendar (no Calendly needed), and live visitor chat directly connected to your phone with zero plugin maintenance.

Can I use my own custom domain with automatic SSL?

+

Yes. BizzQ supports full custom apex domains and subdomains (e.g. yourstudio.com) with automated SSL encryption certificates and global CDN edge routing for lightning-fast international delivery.
