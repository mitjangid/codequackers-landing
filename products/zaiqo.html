Engineered for Kitchen Speed & Front-of-House Precision

⚡

Sub-100ms KOT Dispatch & Live KDS

Instant ticket transmission to kitchen printers and digital Kitchen Display Systems (KDS). Line-item cooking notes, course pacing, and zero lost tickets during peak meal rushes.

📲

Guest Dine-in Table QR Ordering

Guests scan unique per-table QR codes with no app download. They browse live menus, send orders straight to the kitchen, and append rounds onto a unified single table bill.

🛵

Cloud Kitchen Webhook Pipeline

Inbound webhook intake for Swiggy and Zomato orders with HMAC signature verification. Manage incoming queues, prep SLAs, rider handoffs, and channel margin reports.

🍱

Tiffin Subscriptions & Meal Plans

Public self-service tiffin booking (`/tiffin`). Guests choose plans and easily pause, resume, or cancel delivery days from their portal while the kitchen receives automated meal tallies.

🏛️

Banquets, Events & Visual Tables

Interactive drag-and-drop floor plan layout with table status flags (free/occupied/reserved). Multi-hall banquet reservation pipeline with clash detection and advance deposits.

🧾

Multi-Tender POS & Flexible Tax Regime

Touch-first cashier and waiter POS with split billing and multi-tender (Cash, UPI, Card). Configure taxes per outlet: Full 5%/18% GST splits, Composition Scheme, or No-GST.
