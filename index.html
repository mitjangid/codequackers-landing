// FAQ

Straight answers. Zero runaround.

Everything you need to know about partnering with CodeQuackers — engineering velocity, IP ownership, tech stack, and sprint cadence.

What services does CodeQuackers provide? +

CodeQuackers is a boutique software studio designing and engineering bespoke web applications, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, real-time telemetry dashboards, React Native mobile apps, and custom operational ERPs. From Day 1 architecture and UI/UX design to production launch, we engineer software built to handle millions of requests without breaking a sweat.

What tech stack does CodeQuackers build with? +

We build with modern, high-performance web standards: React, Next.js, TypeScript, React Native, Node.js, Python, Golang, Laravel, PostgreSQL, Redis, Tailwind CSS, Docker, and Cloudflare. We select the optimal stack tailored to your product's latency, concurrency, and scaling demands.

What production products has CodeQuackers built? +

Ten live platforms run on CodeQuackers engineering: Rajaram Marmo (enterprise stone manufacturing ERP), Zaiqo (restaurant ERP & POS), BillNBin (multi-tenant inventory & GST billing), BhavTrack (live bullion rates & WebSockets trading), PowerTrack (gym management & biometric CRM), AquaTrack (water dispenser logistics), LabTrack (pathology lab LIS), BizzQ (business website builder & CMS), Reservo (sports facility booking), and Nocturne (bar & nightclub POS). Plus, two major platforms are currently in private beta: Adhiti (institute ERP) and BytePress (digital news portal engine).

How fast can you build and launch an MVP or SaaS platform? +

Our focused MVP sprints ship in 10 to 14 days, while comprehensive multi-module enterprise platforms typically launch in 3 to 5 weeks. By pairing senior product architects with advanced AI orchestration, we eliminate boilerplate lag and deliver production-ready code at unprecedented velocity.

How does CodeQuackers price projects and collaborate? +

We provide transparent fixed sprint pricing for well-defined builds and monthly retainers for ongoing feature velocity. You collaborate directly with senior product engineers with zero agency bureaucracy, no account managers playing telephone, and async updates in Slack or Discord.

Does CodeQuackers work with international clients outside India? +

Yes. While based in India, we partner with founders and companies across the US, UK, Europe, UAE, and Southeast Asia. We communicate asynchronously, maintain flexible meeting overlaps across timezones, and support seamless international billing.
