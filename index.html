FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about partnering with CodeQuackers — from project scoping to shipping and post-launch maintenance.

What services does CodeQuackers provide? +

CodeQuackers designs and engineers bespoke web applications, SaaS platforms, fintech software, real-time analytics dashboards, and custom ERP systems. From initial product architecture and UI/UX design to production launch and ongoing cloud maintenance, we build dependable, high-speed software tailored to your business needs.

What tech stack does CodeQuackers build with? +

We build with modern, high-performance web technologies including React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, Redis, Tailwind CSS, and cloud-native infrastructure on AWS, Cloudflare, and Vercel. We select the ideal architectural stack for each project's speed, security, and scalability requirements.

What production products has CodeQuackers built? +

Nine production platforms are live in the wild: Zaiqo (restaurant ERP & POS), BillNBin (multi-tenant inventory & GST billing), BhavTrack (live bullion rates & trading), PowerTrack (gym management), AquaTrack (water dispenser logistics), LabTrack (pathology lab management), BizzQ (websites, blogs & booking), Reservo (facility booking), and Nocturne (bar & club management). Plus, two major upcoming platforms are currently in active development: Adhiti (an all-in-one 76-module school and institute ERP) and BytePress (a high-performance digital news portal built with Astro & Go).

How fast can you build and launch an MVP or SaaS platform? +

Most MVPs ship in 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope and complexity. Our AI-accelerated engineering workflows, reusable architectural components, and tight feedback loops enable us to deliver production-ready software in weeks rather than quarters.

How does CodeQuackers price projects and collaborate with clients? +

We offer transparent, milestone-based pricing for well-defined scopes and dedicated monthly sprint retainers for evolving platforms. Clients work directly with the senior engineers building their product with zero agency bureaucracy or communication lag.

Does CodeQuackers work with international clients outside India? +

Yes! While our studio is based in India, we partner with founders, startups, and companies across the US, UK, UAE, and Southeast Asia. We support asynchronous communication, flexible meeting schedules across timezones, and smooth international invoicing.
