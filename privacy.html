Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. Short version: we collect only what we need to talk to you, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message, when you reach out to us.
  • Basic usage data: if we enable a privacy-friendly analytics counter on this page, it records aggregated, anonymised totals (pages viewed, approximate region) purely to see how the site is doing. It never profiles you, never follows you across sites and never collects personal identifiers.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and project requests.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations where they apply.

Cookies & third parties

We don't set tracking cookies and we don't run ads or cross-site trackers. Fonts are self-hosted from our own domain. If a privacy-friendly analytics counter is enabled, it works without cookies and without fingerprinting. Our product sites are separate apps with their own policies.

Sharing your data

We never sell your personal information. We only share it with the service providers we use to run our business (such as email and hosting), and only to the extent needed to provide our services.

Data retention

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them once they're no longer useful.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time. Just email codequackers.in@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date above.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests? Reach us at codequackers.in@gmail.com.
